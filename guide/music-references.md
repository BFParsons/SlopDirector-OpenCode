# Music references by director style

Researched 9 September 2026. Scorsese added 10 September 2026. Covers all 43 named SlopStudio styles with 103 entries: individual tracks, complete scores, creator catalogues and explicitly labelled sound references. This is a representative listening library, not an exhaustive filmography.

Each table records the music, artist or composer, associated work, kind of association and a source. A film score, trailer cover, directed music video and creator-curated playlist are different kinds of evidence. Catalogue listening leads do not establish use in an individual video. Unconfirmed credits are stated explicitly. Listening directions are editorial suggestions for this harness, not claims about the creator's intentions.

For future plans: select by the story's emotional purpose, audition the exact recording and passage, then name the intended entry, exit and transition. Keep narration and music on separate tracks. Music heard within the scene must retain its relationship to that scene. References do not override a style's preference for silence or dry location sound. This library records associations, not music files or clearance status; follow the user's project sourcing brief.

The matching section is included automatically in `get_style`. House style has no named director and uses the project's own music brief.
