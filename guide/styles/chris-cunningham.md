# Chris Cunningham

## Reference and mechanism

Music-dependent bodily and mechanical transformation; Rubber Johnny is a specific reference.

An apparently physical body behaves according to an unfamiliar musical logic.

## Evidence

Cunningham describes his visual tone as dependent on the music and discusses Rubber Johnny's prolonged editing. The film's night-vision imagery directly challenges the harness's daylight-only rule. His stated interest in ambient music also complicates an exclusively aggressive stereotype.

**Basis:** Direct craft account. The production choices below are editorial translations, not measured career-wide rules.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Requires planned production. Precisely controllable movement, convincing bodily or mechanical detail and a suitable musical structure.

## Picture and clip selection

Gather a recognizable baseline movement before its distortion. Choose joints, contact points and materials that make the transformation readable instead of relying on vague grotesque imagery.

## Framing and camera

Keep an anatomical or mechanical anchor visible through the effect. Choose clinical, nocturnal or other lighting from the specific reference, with enough detail to judge the illusion.

## Structure and rhythm

Test a repeated movement first at natural timing, then against a changed musical subdivision. Escalate the rule's consequences; include a sustained moment where viewers can inspect what changed.

## Narration and sound

Choose music whose texture supports the intended bodily response. Coordinate transient detail with motion, but reserve some continuous sound to prevent every edit feeling identical.

## Type

Avoid explanatory typography during a perceptual reveal. Use a restrained identifying frame only where the piece needs one.

Bundled typography presets are starting treatments. Choose a named work and a coherent project-specific title and supporting-text system. Fonts, colors, cases and roles may vary; source labels and accessibility captions remain available. Check safe areas, reading time and overlap.

## Exceptions and review

Replace mandatory daylight, fixed discomfort holds and universal horror cues with a stated sensory premise. Effects require a baseline against which the disturbance can be perceived.

Numeric pacing and narration ranges in the catalogue are editorial starting points, not measurements of the creator's career. Explain purposeful departures in styleTreatment.exceptions. Source integrity and technical validity still apply.

## Evaluation

Make a 16-second normal-to-transformed movement study. Viewers should identify both the original motion and the new rule governing it.

**Failure mode:** Glitch overlays and fast cuts conceal rather than produce an uncanny physical idea.

The duration above is a suggested experiment length. Plan the test within the approved production scope; it is not a new approval gate or a claim that the test has passed.
