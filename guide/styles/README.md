# Directing references

Choose a named work, campaign or period, then adapt its mechanism to the available material. Read [Applying a director reference](../playbooks/director-style.md). The 43 researched profiles include sources, material prerequisites, production choices, exceptions and an observable evaluation.

Catalogue values are editorial starting defaults. Creative departures receive review warnings; technical and factual requirements remain. Store the project-specific treatment in plan.styleTreatment. Typography presets are adaptable treatments, not universal fonts or forbidden roles.

## Documentary

- [Adam Curtis](adam-curtis.md) — the archive essay: raided archive, an essayist's narration, found music against the picture, hard cuts to black
- [Michael Moore](michael-moore.md) — A public claim becomes a concrete, often absurd encounter.
- [Errol Morris](errol-morris.md) — Testimony and recurring images expose uncertainty instead of closing it too soon.
- [Ken Burns](ken-burns.md) — An image changes meaning as its context and human stakes become visible.
- [Werner Herzog](werner-herzog.md) — A concrete observation opens a larger, unsettling question.
- [Frederick Wiseman](frederick-wiseman.md) — Procedures and relationships reveal how an institution works.
- [Humphrey Jennings](humphrey-jennings.md) — Sound connects separate lives into a larger social picture.
- [LEMMiNO](lemmino.md) — A confusing event becomes a set of inspectable claims, positions and uncertainties.

## Advertising

## Dramatic scripted

- [Christopher Nolan](christopher-nolan.md) — Separate strands acquire urgency as their relationship becomes intelligible.
- [Denis Villeneuve](denis-villeneuve.md) — A small human perception or gesture gains force against a vast environment.

- [Terrence Malick](terrence-malick.md) — Image, gesture and voice suggest an experience larger than any literal illustration.

## Adding or revising a reference

Keep the stable id and genre routing in src/lib/styles/index.ts. For a researched reference, add its craft fields and source records in src/lib/styles/research.json and matching prose in guide/styles/<id>.md. State evidence limitations. The catalogue exposes the mechanism and production fit; get_style exposes the complete craft record and shared playbook. Add a regression check for any behavior change.

The atlas remains a dated research artifact; the runtime profile files and structured craft records are the implemented guidance.
