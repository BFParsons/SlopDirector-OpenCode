# Frederick Wiseman

## Reference and mechanism

Institutional observational documentary; At Berkeley and Boxing Gym provide concrete editing references.

Procedures and relationships reveal how an institution works.

## Evidence

Wiseman describes reducing a lengthy class to a much shorter constructed scene and linking Boxing Gym sequences with overlapping location sound. Observational presentation therefore coexists with substantial editorial construction.

**Basis:** Direct craft account. The production choices below are editorial translations, not measured career-wide rules.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Needs specific source footage. Complete interactions, intelligible location audio, procedural coverage and context for the participants' roles.

## Picture and clip selection

Find a task with a decision, negotiation or change in authority. Favor exchanges that show the rules operating over isolated emotional reactions.

## Framing and camera

Preserve who speaks to whom and what each person is doing. Include spatial or procedural context before relying on a revealing close-up.

## Structure and rhythm

Compress repetition while maintaining the logic of the exchange. Join scenes because their procedures, power relations or sounds converse; retain ambiguity where the institution itself produces it.

## Narration and sound

Use location sound to carry continuity or connect different activities. Avoid adding sounds that misrepresent what happened; silence and room tone can also separate institutional spaces.

## Type

Keep administrative context available in restrained labels when necessary. A title may orient viewers without telling them which participant is right.

Bundled typography presets are starting treatments. Choose a named work and a coherent project-specific title and supporting-text system. Fonts, colors, cases and roles may vary; source labels and accessibility captions remain available. Check safe areas, reading time and overlap.

## Exceptions and review

An observational reference can prefer no explanatory narrator or added score. Remove the claim that statements cannot be shortened: ethical compression and constructed continuity are central editorial decisions.

Numeric pacing and narration ranges in the catalogue are editorial starting points, not measurements of the creator's career. Explain purposeful departures in styleTreatment.exceptions. Source integrity and technical validity still apply.

## Evaluation

Cut a 30-second procedural scene. A viewer should identify the task, the decision-maker and the unresolved tension without voiceover.

**Failure mode:** Long shots of an institution with no intelligible process produce opacity rather than observation.

The duration above is a suggested experiment length. Plan the test within the approved production scope; it is not a new approval gate or a claim that the test has passed.
