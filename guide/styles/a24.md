# A24 — the cryptic teaser

Work: trailers and teasers for *Hereditary*, *Midsommar*, *The Lighthouse*, *Uncut Gems*, *Everything Everywhere All at Once*, *Past Lives*, *The Zone of Interest*, *Civil War*; the A24 in-house marketing look.

## The signature

Shows almost everything, tells almost nothing. A trailer with fewer transitions of thought — one mood, one sound, one typographic idea; a title treatment that draws itself; a sound design (a drone, a breath, a folk song, a needle-drop) doing the work a narrator would. The film is sold as an object: the type, the colour, the texture of the footage. You leave knowing how it feels, not what happens.

## Structure — how a teaser is built

- One line of thought: a single situation shown in fragments; a single sound that evolves; the type as punctuation (a word, then the title).
- 60–90 s: the images arrive in order of intimacy (a landscape, a house, a face, an eye); the sound starts as ambience and becomes music or a scream; the title is the last image, sometimes silent.
- No cards of praise ("From the visionary director…"), or one, ironic; no premise card.

## The cut

- Average shot 2–5 s; holds of 6–8 s on a face or a landscape; a burst of 0.5–1 s shots at the peak, if any; nothing under 0.4 s.
- Straight cuts; cuts to the type on white or black; a match cut once (a shape into a shape); no dissolves.
- The type is a shot: the word held 2–3 s, clean, on a flat colour; the title treatment animates once (draws, widens, breathes).
- Grade: the film's exact look; no sweetening; grain and format (4:3, 1.66, 2.39) kept as the film has them.

## Narration

- None. One or two lines of dialogue, or a voice from a song; often nothing spoken at all.

## Sound

- Music is **specific**: a folk song, a hymn, a needle-drop from the film, a single instrument, or no music — a drone and the film's own sound design (search "folk hymn a cappella CC", "solo cello drone library", "field recording ambience"); one sound idea, evolving.
- Sync sound is the film's: a door, a breath, an insect, a crowd; treated as music (audio-only clips placed on the cuts); silence held 1–2 s before the title.
- No braam, no riser, no hit on the title unless the film is loud.

## Picture — sources and text

- Sources: the film's footage; for a synthetic teaser, generated shots with one strong texture ("16 mm, 4:3, overcast field, a woman in a white dress looking at something off frame, grain, muted greens").
- Text: the type is the identity — a single typeface, chosen for the film (a serif for a period piece, a condensed grotesk for a thriller, a hand-drawn face for a horror), white or black on a flat colour, words held alone (2–3 s); the title treatment 4–5 s; a date in the same face.

## Do not

- No narrator, no premise card, no montage of "moments", no braam, no riser, no lower-third, no review quotes (or one, dry).
- No trailer-house grade; no dissolves; no more than one typographic idea.
- No shot that explains the previous one.

## Type

**The signature.** One typeface chosen per film — a clean serif (Hereditary), a delicate one (Midsommar), a rugged bold face (The Lighthouse) — words held alone on a flat field; the type is the identity.

**Stand-ins.** Playfair Display for the film serifs; Jost for a geometric when the film wants one. Faces: Playfair Display 400, Jost 400. Case: sentence. Colour #FFFFFF on #0E0E0E. Entrance: none.

**Roles** (`add_text_overlay {role}` with this style in the brief):

- **card (default)** — a single word or line on the flat field. Playfair Display · 5.5 % · none · hold 2.5 s.
- **title** — the title treatment, the last image. Playfair Display · spaced capitals · 7 % · none · hold 4.5 s.
- **date** — the date in the same manner. Jost · capitals · 3.4 % · center · none.

**Never:** lower-third, caption, callout, citation, label, intertitle.

**Survey note.** Surveyed: A24's trailers pick a serif or a display face per film and keep to one typographic idea; Playfair stands in for the serifs.

## Harness parameters

asl 2–5 s (holds 6–8 s) · min shot 0.4 s · transitions cuts (type on flat colour) · narration none · music optional, specific (folk / drone / needle-drop) · sync mixed · text cards (typographic) · interviews none · beat-cut no

## Applying it in SlopStudio

- Plan: one beat of fragments in order of intimacy; `card` shots with single words at the punctuation points; the title card last; `music` may be null with a designed ambience instead.
- Cut: `add_text_overlay` with one typeface throughout (style BOX on a flat field or a `card` shot); `update_project` grade untouched; `check_soundtrack` will note "no bed" if the sound is designed — the style.
- Music: `set_music` the folk song or `add_segment audioOnly` the drone and the film's sounds; `balance_music` gap 4.
