# A24

## Reference and mechanism

A studio and distributor; define the actual film campaign and credited agency before prescribing craft.

Campaign specificity is the useful starting point; there is no defensible single-director system.

## Evidence

The Hereditary 'Charlie' credit record identifies A24 as studio, Buddha Jones as agency, Tommy Malatesta as producer/editor, Brent Buckman as creative director, Eric Peschel on graphics and AVS Music. It provides a concrete attribution correction, not proof of a universal A24 editing formula.

**Basis:** Primary campaign credit; broader style claim unsupported. The production choices below are editorial translations, not measured career-wide rules.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Archive-compatible. One named trailer version with credits, its film-specific hook and footage that supports that promise.

## Picture and clip selection

Write the campaign's promise in one sentence, then select a distinctive character, object or situation that can carry it. Separate an intriguing omission from missing basic premise information.

## Framing and camera

Choose a consistent image logic from the film itself: intimacy, geometry, texture or scale. Do not apply a studio-wide palette to incompatible source footage.

## Structure and rhythm

Develop one concrete reference version. Test how much orientation viewers need before ambiguity becomes productive; let pace follow the campaign rather than an assumed 'art-house' duration.

## Narration and sound

Choose a sonic identity grounded in that film's material or campaign concept. Record which cue, motif and silence pattern belong to the reference instead of calling them A24 defaults.

## Type

Research the specific campaign's title and supporting cards. Allow premise lines, reviews and multiple typographic roles when they have a clear purpose.

Bundled typography presets are starting treatments. Choose a named work and a coherent project-specific title and supporting-text system. Fonts, colors, cases and roles may vary; source labels and accessibility captions remain available. Check safe areas, reading time and overlap.

## Exceptions and review

Reclassify as a campaign collection or studio filter. Remove blanket bans on voiceover, explanations, montage and card counts; attach actual agency and version metadata.

Numeric pacing and narration ranges in the catalogue are editorial starting points, not measurements of the creator's career. Explain purposeful departures in styleTreatment.exceptions. Source integrity and technical validity still apply.

## Evaluation

Make a 20-second concept whose film-specific promise remains clear after hiding the studio logo. If the only identifiable attribute is the logo, the profile is not specific enough.

**Failure mode:** Sparse cards and eerie music can imitate a marketing stereotype without capturing any particular campaign.

The duration above is a suggested experiment length. Plan the test within the approved production scope; it is not a new approval gate or a claim that the test has passed.
