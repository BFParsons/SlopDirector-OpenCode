# Anaïs Bimpel — sound-led trailer construction

A sound-led trailer can organize selected actions, dialogue and musical phrases
into a distinctive rhythm. This is a creative approach to explore, not a claim
that every Anaïs Bimpel campaign uses one construction.
Her [portfolio and credits](https://anaisb.com/awards-press) include *Daughters*,
*Life on Our Planet* and *The Handmaiden*.

## Creative constraints

Follow [Trailer construction](../playbooks/trailer-construction.md). No fixed
part lengths, mandatory tempo doubling, delayed music entrance, one-bar silence,
or one-to-one relationship between sounds and picture cuts is required.

## Select and build

- Establish the character, situation and change in pressure before making a
  percussion pattern. Choose actions and lines that mean something in the story.
- Log each usable source sound with its exact moment and handles. Distinguish
  authentic sync from extracted sound and added editorial design.
- Test a small sound-led sequence. A gesture, word or effect may accent the
  rhythm within a held shot; another cut may anticipate or follow the accent.
- Give dialogue and reactions enough space to remain understandable. Let longer
  holds interrupt a cluster when that helps a joke, emotion or transition.

## Music and sound

Audition the actual recording and passage for attitude and arrangement options.
Find a tempo that serves the performance and selected music; do not choose a cue
only to fit an arbitrary grid. Music may enter immediately, gradually or late.
Narration, sustained score and quiet passages are available if the concept calls
for them.

Keep music, dialogue, scene effects and ambience separately controllable.
Choose a foreground element for each beat; vary density and preserve useful
tails. Review music joins, time stretching, timing and intelligibility by
listening. Measurements alone do not establish a finished soundtrack.

## Picture and typography

Straight cuts are a useful starting point; use other transitions deliberately.
Preserve source quality and the campaign's visual identity. Design a distinct
title treatment and supporting text; cards can sit over picture when legible.
Oswald Bold and the existing pop presets are available stand-ins, not verified
universal choices. There is no fixed card count, word count, or required title hit.

## Harness parameters

ASL 1–3 s as a review reference; minimum style reference 0.25 s; cuts as a default;
narration optional; music recommended; mixed sync; cards optional; beat-grid
verification only for passages designed around a grid. Technical frame and
delivery checks still apply.
