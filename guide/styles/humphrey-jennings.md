# Humphrey Jennings

## Reference and mechanism

Poetic wartime documentary; distinguish Listen to Britain from the narrated Words for Battle.

Sound connects separate lives into a larger social picture.

## Evidence

BFI's account distinguishes Jennings's different forms and identifies Words for Battle's narration. Listen to Britain is also a collaboration with Stewart McAllister; its achievement should not be reduced to a sole-director visual preset.

**Basis:** Institutional analysis and primary work record. The production choices below are editorial translations, not measured career-wide rules.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Archive-compatible. Everyday activities with useful sound, contrasting social spaces and reliable historical identification.

## Picture and clip selection

Pair work, leisure and public ritual through a shared action or sound. Look for social difference within the connection, rather than smoothing every image into national unity.

## Framing and camera

Retain enough of each environment to make its social role visible. Human activity and the surrounding space should both contribute to the association.

## Structure and rhythm

Try introducing a sound in one place and continuing it over another. Return to its source after the connection becomes clear; vary the distance between sonic and visual links.

## Narration and sound

Plan the sequence from listening relationships: machinery, footsteps, broadcast, communal music, quiet. Treat recorded music within the scene differently from an added emotional bed.

## Type

Use contextual dates or locations where archive alone is insufficient. Title design should support the chosen historical execution, not force all work into one typography rule.

Bundled typography presets are starting treatments. Choose a named work and a coherent project-specific title and supporting-text system. Fonts, colors, cases and roles may vary; source labels and accessibility captions remain available. Check safe areas, reading time and overlap.

## Exceptions and review

The prohibition on commentary is contradicted by Words for Battle. Keep sound-led association as a strong option, while allowing visual links and different forms across the career.

Numeric pacing and narration ranges in the catalogue are editorial starting points, not measurements of the creator's career. Explain purposeful departures in styleTreatment.exceptions. Source integrity and technical validity still apply.

## Evaluation

Make a 25-second sequence connecting three spaces through one sound. Viewers should identify both the connection and how the spaces differ.

**Failure mode:** A patriotic music bed over assorted archive erases the specific relationships that make the montage meaningful.

The duration above is a suggested experiment length. Plan the test within the approved production scope; it is not a new approval gate or a claim that the test has passed.
