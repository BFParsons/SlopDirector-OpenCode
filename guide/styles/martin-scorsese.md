# Martin Scorsese

## Reference and mechanism

Goodfellas and Casino for insider seduction, social machinery and unraveling; The Irishman for retrospective restraint. Select one film/sequence as the governing reference. Credit editor Thelma Schoonmaker and the relevant camera, sound, music and title collaborators.

An insider's account draws us into a world of status and appetite, then behavior and consequences expose the cost.

## Evidence and scope

Schoonmaker describes The Irishman's deliberate simplicity and music experiments. Fleischman details Goodfellas foreground sound handoffs. Ballhaus and McConkey describe spatial planning and camera collaboration. The Casino title record credits Saul and Elaine Bass. The harness mechanism and exercise are editorial translations of these accounts.

**Basis:** Primary collaborator interviews and reproduced production/title records; sequence-specific evidence. Production recipes and numeric defaults are editorial proposals, not measured career-wide laws.

Use *Goodfellas*' access and status sequences as a starting reference for a rise-and-fall story. Choose *The Irishman* when the point is memory, quiet control or the residue of a life. *The Age of Innocence* is a reminder that this approach can examine social rules outside the crime genre. Do not blend these modes automatically.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Archive-compatible with character and consequence coverage; choreographed camera passages need suitable source footage or a shoot.

Log a protagonist's desire, an actual transaction or ritual granting access, a change in a relationship, and its visible cost. For archive, retain dates and speaker context. For a sustained moving shot, secure a readable route, blocking, permissions, lighting and sound; a digital push-in cannot supply missing space.

## Picture and clip selection

Tag selects as invitation, privilege, procedure, fracture or aftermath. Favor a telling exchange, money or object changing hands, someone granting access, or a listener reassessing a speaker. Retain performance turns and ordinary labor that explain how the world works. Glamour and consequences must both have concrete footage.

In a selects log, record source in/out, the character's objective, what changes within the shot, the available sound and the reason for its dramatic category. Reject interchangeable luxury, city or violence inserts when they explain no relationship.

## Framing and camera

Use proximity, eyelines, doorways and foreground obstacles to show who belongs and who controls access. For new footage, choreograph a move around changing relationships and rehearse the complete route. With existing clips, preserve their screen direction and spatial anchors. Choose light, lens contrast and palette from the named film; red light is optional.

The Copacabana reference is a coordinated passage through social spaces, involving Michael Ballhaus, Larry McConkey and the production team. Existing fragments may express entry and privilege through coherent cuts; do not promise an unbroken traversal that the footage cannot provide.

## Structure and rhythm

Build an inviting scene, compress repeated procedures, then let a specific hesitation or refusal interrupt the momentum. Vary complete dialogue scenes with associative montage. A freeze, repeated detail, temporal jump or abrupt cut needs a stated narrative job and readable duration. Preserve performance over cosmetic continuity when justified. For The Irishman, allow quiet and aftermath to dominate rather than importing Goodfellas velocity.

For the energetic mode, try: invitation into the world -> a concrete benefit -> the routine sustaining it -> a fracture -> an aftermath that revises the opening. These are dramatic functions, not fixed act lengths. A held two-person scene may carry a larger change than a montage.

For retrospective restraint, begin with a present-tense condition, move to a specific memory, then return with altered meaning. Establish the temporal anchor before asking viewers to follow a jump.

## Narration, music and sound

Optional first-person narration can give a character's self-justification while picture reveals a discrepancy; write concrete recollection and avoid imitating an actor's voice. Use verified quotes for factual subjects. Audition exact song recordings/passages for emotional subtext, period and structural turns; song, score or unscored scene are valid. Map which line, lyric, practical sound or musical phrase leads each moment, with separate stems and deliberate quiet after consequences.

Choose a performance direction such as conversational recollection, defensive explanation or rueful recognition. Write what the speaker thinks happened; let a specific image or response test that belief. Identify the true closing line and allow its final thought and natural tail to settle.

Write a music-event map: exact recording and passage, why it enters, what it initially promises, what changes its meaning, where dialogue takes over, and how it exits. The associated music library supplies film-song references, not pre-cleared trailer cues. A familiar song alone is not the mechanism.

## Type

Design the film title as a story-specific metaphor or identity, separately from supporting dates, names and quotes. Credit Saul and Elaine Bass when referencing their Casino sequence. Bundled bold sans and restrained serif treatments are editorial options, not verified Scorsese-wide fonts. Animate only when motion expresses the chosen idea; hold source labels long enough to read.

Starting treatment: a distinct white title in Montserrat SemiBold on a dark field; plain Liberation Sans supporting information; an EB Garamond option for a more reflective period treatment. These are bundled design choices, not an attribution of those fonts to the filmmaker. Use the selected film's title logic when another treatment fits.

## Exceptions and review

This profile also supports restrained character drama and period work. Narration, pop songs, freeze frames, violence, red lighting and long tracking shots are optional. A trailer needs its own campaign construction and named trailer execution where available; a film-director reference is not a trailer-agency credit. Preserve factual integrity when translating the mechanism to documentary material.

Average shot length 2-6 seconds, 0.33-second minimum style reference, straight cuts, optional first-person narration and optional found music are draft review defaults. Narration WPM 70-120 describes a possible spoken passage, not a quota across the film. Technical frame, source, delivery and readability requirements remain in effect. Long holds and shorter legible accents can be motivated departures.

## Evaluation

Make a 20-30-second proof with an invitation, a visible privilege or transaction, and a consequence. A viewer should identify whose perspective shaped the opening, who held power, and what changed. Test a song-led and an unscored turn; inspect geography, full dialogue, title legibility and the final hold. Report listening and material limitations separately.

**Failure mode:** A rapid gangster montage with a familiar rock song supplies surface attitude but no social mechanism, changing point of view or cost.

The proof is an exercise inside the user's authorized production scope. Its proposed duration is not a mandatory new approval gate or evidence that this reference has already passed an audience test.
