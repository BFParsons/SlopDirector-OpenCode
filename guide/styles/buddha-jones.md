# Buddha Jones

## Reference and mechanism

A trailer agency with multiple teams and campaigns; use Bill Neil's craft discussion and credited examples, not a single horror formula.

Controlled information and changing sonic pressure build anticipation and reversal.

## Evidence

Vox's Bill Neil interview description explicitly identifies sound design and rug pulls as trailer techniques. The Clio record supplies a specific Buddha Jones campaign reference for Boots. Neither source establishes that every agency trailer must cut to black or hide every reveal.

**Basis:** Primary interview synopsis and campaign credit; detailed timing remains to be measured. The production choices below are editorial translations, not measured career-wide rules.

## Material prerequisites

**Production fit:** Archive-compatible. A legible hook, escalating source events, a strong reveal or reversal and independently editable dialogue, music and effects.

## Picture and clip selection

Rank clips by what they disclose: rule, threat, reaction, escalation or payoff. Choose one distinctive set piece for identity and enough connective material to make its stakes readable.

## Framing and camera

Protect spatial orientation before a surprise. Select approach and reaction coverage that can sustain expectation without relying entirely on black frames.

## Structure and rhythm

Try a complete miniature scene as the opening, then a change of scale and a compressed final passage. Reserve a reversal or button for after the audience thinks the section has ended.

## Narration and sound

Build separate tracks for atmosphere, pulse, transitions, impacts and dialogue. Design contrast in density and silence; derive a recurring motif from the film when the source supports it.

## Type

Give cards, title and end information distinct jobs. Synchronize a title entrance to a chosen event rather than adding a hit to every text appearance.

Bundled typography presets are starting treatments. Choose a named work and a coherent project-specific title and supporting-text system. Fonts, colors, cases and roles may vary; source labels and accessibility captions remain available. Check safe areas, reading time and overlap.

## Exceptions and review

Replace universal horror rules with campaign-specific reveal strategy. Allow finished actions, warmth, narration and non-black transitions where they strengthen the promise.

Numeric pacing and narration ranges in the catalogue are editorial starting points, not measurements of the creator's career. Explain purposeful departures in styleTreatment.exceptions. Source integrity and technical validity still apply.

## Evaluation

Build a 30-second trailer passage with one deliberate pressure release and reversal. Viewers should anticipate one event and clearly register the changed expectation.

**Failure mode:** Continuous risers and black-frame impacts exhaust contrast before the important reveal.

The duration above is a suggested experiment length. Plan the test within the approved production scope; it is not a new approval gate or a claim that the test has passed.
