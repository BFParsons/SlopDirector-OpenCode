# Clean dialogue from film clips

Applies to every style and project reusing finished film, television, trailer or other scored footage. A dialogue clip can contain both speech and music. Speech detection and good LUFS measurements do not establish a clean source.

1. Log every retained dialogue excerpt with source asset and exact in/out points. Audition it solo, including the handles and gaps between words. Classify embedded music as none, present or unknown. Prefer a suitable music-free line or a genuine dialogue stem when available. Keep all visual-only film clips muted.
2. If music is present, select a clean alternative or separate the voice from accompaniment. Preserve source identity, wording, sync and timing. A center channel may also contain score and is only a candidate. EQ, denoising, lowering clip volume and ducking the new score do not remove an existing score.
3. For local separation, use the installed Demucs workflow (`src/lib/audio/demucs.ts`, Audio Studio stems API) or another available dialogue isolator. Extract with handles, separate, then trim to the exact picture interval. Keep the original, the voice stem and removed accompaniment for comparison. The model's `vocals` label is not proof of a music-free or undamaged voice.
4. Audition original and isolated versions level-matched: solo, in the intended mix, and across each entrance/exit. Check residual melody, bass and percussion, musical tails, missing consonants, watery speech, pumping, phase and lip sync. If cleanup damages the line or leaves clashing music, select another excerpt or drop the line. Do not hide it by adding more score.
5. Mute the original picture's audio when adding a voice stem. Place dialogue, music and effects on separate tracks. Preserve breaths and word boundaries, use short edge fades, level dialogue, then arrange/duck the cue around it. Confirm that the original soundtrack is not playing twice.
6. Store `plan.shots[].sourceAudio`: `music` is the selected audio's none/present/unknown status; `treatment` is original/isolated/replacement; `review` is pending/listened; bind `assetId`, `startS`, `endS` to the audible source interval. Optionally record `originalMusic` and cleanup/listening notes. With isolated audio, these points belong to the stem, while shot.source remains the picture. Never write listened or none from a transcript, meter or completed separation job.
7. Run check_plan, check_soundtrack and check_mix_levels, then review the rendered excerpt by listening. Known embedded music fails the source audit; unknown/unreviewed or stale asset/range assessments remain warnings. A mechanical `pass` means no detected errors, not listening approval. If listening is unavailable, deliver only as a review candidate with the unresolved check stated; do not call the mix verified clean.

These requirements apply equally to A24, Scorsese and every other director reference. Intentional on-screen musical performance needs a separately stated scene-sound decision; do not silently treat a scored dialogue clip as clean.
