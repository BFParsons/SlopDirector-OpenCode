# Applying a director reference

**Goal.** Make the selected creative mechanism perceptible with the available material. A director, creator, agency or studio label is a starting reference; name the work, period or campaign version and relevant collaborators.

## Evidence and choice

Read the selected profile's evidence and material prerequisites before designing the shots. Distinguish documented practice from the harness's production suggestions. Catalogue pacing, narration, music and typography parameters are editorial starting defaults, not measured career-wide prohibitions. Creative departures are review findings; source integrity, technical validity and explicit user constraints remain requirements.

For agencies and studios, identify the actual campaign. A24 is a studio/distributor, not a single trailer director. Every Frame a Painting credits Taylor Ramos and Tony Zhou. A fixed font, grade, shot duration or transition does not establish a filmmaker's style.

## Plan the treatment

After the interview, store these fields in plan.styleTreatment. Use existing answers and delegated choices; this adds no interview or approval round.

- reference: the named work/version or bounded period, plus collaborators where relevant. Identify uncertainty if an exact version is unavailable.
- mechanism: what the viewer should perceive: a contradiction, discovery, musical pattern, status change, conceptual relationship or trailer promise.
- materialPlan: the coverage or evidence needed, what is available, what must be sourced or produced, and the fallback if it cannot be obtained.
- rhythm: the section-level pattern of orientation, development, contrast and payoff. Specify selected accents or held actions; do not require every cut to hit a grid.
- sound: dialogue priority, music's role and exact passage when known, environment, practical sounds, transitions and planned silence. Audition before locking detailed timing.
- typography: separate the film title from supporting text; choose roles, reading purpose, source labels and reveal behavior. Bundled presets are adaptable starting points.
- exceptions: purposeful departures from reference defaults and their intended benefit. An empty list means none are currently planned.
- evaluation: a bounded proof or review of a relevant passage, with an observable result. Record what the viewer should understand or anticipate, not just a target name.

Carry the treatment into the beats, shot descriptions, clip queries, durations, script offsets and sound decisions. A filled-out treatment is not proof the edit achieves it. check_plan warns when a researched profile lacks a treatment; older plans remain readable.

## Match the material

Archive-compatible means the edit can work if the right evidence and moments exist. Interview and observational profiles need intelligible exchanges, procedural context or meaningful gestures. Planned staging, choreography, camera relationships and physical transformations need footage containing those relationships. A crop, LUT, zoom or whoosh cannot supply missing performance.

Diagrams must distinguish known, inferred and unknown information. Do not invent failures for an engineering story, reactions for a political comparison, or claims for a documentary. Keep dates, qualifications and reconstruction status intact. For archival trailers, combine a clear trailer promise with expressive techniques the selects can sustain; read trailer-construction as well.

## Evaluate the result

Use the profile's microtest as a suggested exercise, not a compulsory separate render for every project. Include new proof production within the approved plan and allowances. A relevant passage in an existing authorized draft may serve the same purpose. Compare a clear baseline with one intentional variation where useful, keeping footage and loudness comparable.

Review the perceptual result: can a viewer identify the relationship, recognize a pattern, understand a mechanism or describe the movie being promised? Record observed strengths, weaknesses and intentional exceptions. Do not manufacture audience results or treat informal responses as statistical validation.

Report four statuses separately: technical checks, picture/motion review, listening review and style evaluation. Use unverified when a review was unavailable. Contact sheets cannot certify motion or timing; a loudness pass cannot certify musical fit, intelligibility or performance. Existing technical and plan-approval requirements still apply.
